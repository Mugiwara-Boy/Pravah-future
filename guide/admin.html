For admins

Admin guide

This guide takes an admin from an empty workspace to a team that is ready to scan. Do steps 1 to 4 once for the organization. Do steps 5 and 6 for every event. It takes about 25 minutes.

Once for your organization

1

Add your team

Reps need an account before they can scan a badge.

  1. Open Team in the left sidebar and click Add member.
  2. Enter the rep's full name and work email. The invitation goes to that address.
  3. Paste their LinkedIn profile link and add their role. The role appears in their email signature.
  4. Choose their access. Booth staff is for reps working the booth. Admin is for people who manage the organization.
  5. Click Add member. They get an email with a link to sign in.

Good to know Click Edit next to a name to update a photo or phone numbers later, or to revoke access when someone leaves. Their conversations and data stay.

Read the narration

Here's how to add a new rep to your team in PravahAI. Start from Team, in the left sidebar.

This page lists everyone in your organization, with their role, their access, and how active they've been. To add someone, click Add member, at the top right.

Enter their full name, and their work email. The invitation goes to this address, so use the one they actually check.

Next, paste their LinkedIn profile link. PravahAI uses it to fetch their profile details.

Add their role, for example Account Executive. It appears in their email signature.

Then choose their access. Booth staff is for reps working the booth. Admin is for people who manage the organization.

Click Add member. They'll get an invitation email with a link to sign in, and they show up in your team list straight away.

To change anything later, click Edit next to their name. You can update their photo and phone numbers here, and revoke their access if they leave. Their conversations and data are kept.

That's it. A new rep is on your team.

2

Describe your business

Every visitor brief and score is written with your business in mind.

  1. Open Settings. Under Business info, paste the link to your website and the links to your product and service pages. Each link starts with https.
  2. Click Enrich, then Save business info. The app pulls the business summary and the products and services from these links.
  3. If your website is out of date, or something was missed, edit the business summary so it matches today. Then click Save summary.
  4. Describe your ideal customer profile. This one comes from you, and it says who you sell to. Name the roles you want to meet and the size of company. Then say who is not a fit. Click Save ideal customer profile.
  5. Open Products and Services. Add any that are missing or out of date, and remove the ones that do not belong.

Good to know The profile is copied to every event you create. To change it for one show, add or remove ideal customer profiles inside that event. Remove it here and new events start without it.

Read the narration

Before your team goes to a show, tell the app what your business sells. Open Settings, in the left sidebar.

Paste the link to your website, and to the pages that describe your product and your services. Each link needs to start with h t t p s.

Click Enrich. The app reads those pages.

Then click Save business info.

We pull the business summary from the links you shared. If your website is out of date, or we missed something, edit the summary here so it matches where you are today. Then click Save summary.

Now the ideal customer profile. This one comes from you. Tell us who you sell to: the roles you want to meet, the company size and the industry, and who is not a fit. Every visitor is scored against it.

Click Save ideal customer profile. It is copied to every event you create. To change it for one show, open that event and add or remove ideal customer profiles there. Remove it here, and new events start without it.

Products and services sit just below. We pull these from your links too. Add any that are missing or out of date, and remove the ones that do not belong.

That's your business set up. Next, the questions your reps should ask.

3

Set the standard questions

Reps see these in order during a conversation, so every visitor gets asked the questions that matter to you.

  1. In Settings, scroll to Standard questions.
  2. Click Add question and type it. Keep each one short and open.
  3. Use the arrows to change the order.
  4. Click Save questions.

Good to know These are the organization's default questions. Each event also has its own list, which you set in step 6.

Read the narration

Standard questions are the ones you want every rep to ask. Set them once for the whole organization, in Settings, further down the page.

Click Add question and type the first one. Keep each question short and open, so the visitor does most of the talking.

Add a few more. For example, how do you follow up with leads after a show today, and who else would be part of a decision like this?

Use the arrows to change the order. Reps see the questions in this order during a conversation.

Click Save questions. These are the organization's default questions. Each event has its own list too, which you set next.

4

Connect your CRM and Slack

Visitors, conversation summaries and meetings go to the system your team already works in.

  1. Open Integrations in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Connect on Salesforce or HubSpot, whichever your team works in.
  3. Click Continue, sign in with your own account and approve the access.
  4. Optional. Connect Slack to get a message in a channel whenever a visitor is added.

Good to know Gmail, Outlook and calendar connections are personal. Each rep connects their own, in step 1 of the booth staff guide. Reps who connect send from their own mailbox and see each email in their Sent folder. Reps who do not connect send from our no reply address, with themselves in CC.

Read the narration

Your C R M comes next. Open Integrations, in the left sidebar.

Connect Salesforce or HubSpot. Pick the one your team already works in. Visitors, conversation summaries and meetings go to it, and existing contacts are matched by email.

Click Connect, then Continue, and sign in with your own account when you're asked. The app never sees your password.

Slack is optional. Connect it if you want a message in a channel whenever a visitor is added.

Gmail, Outlook and calendar connections are personal. Each rep connects their own, and the booth staff guide covers that.

For every event

5

Create the event

Each show gets its own workspace, with its own visitors, questions and results.

  1. Open Events and click Create event.
  2. Enter the event name and the city, then choose the time zone.
  3. Click Dates, pick the first day and the last day, then set the start and end times.
  4. Add a description with your booth goals and what the team should track.
  5. Add the industries you are targeting. The ideal customer profile is copied from your organization settings. Add or remove ideal customer profiles here for this show only.
  6. Click Create event.

Good to know The event page stays empty until the first visitor is added.

Read the narration

Now create the event. Open Events, then click Create event.

Type the event name and the city.

Choose the time zone. Dates and times are in the event's local time.

Click the dates field. Pick the first day, then the last day. Then set the start and end times.

Add a description, with your booth goals and what the team should track.

Under business info, add the industries you are targeting at this show. The ideal customer profile is copied from your organization settings. For this show, add or remove ideal customer profiles here. The organization setting stays as it is.

Click Create event. You land on the event's page, which stays empty until your first visitor is added.

6

Set the event's questions and scan defaults

This decides what reps are asked to cover at this show, and how much is looked up for each visitor.

  1. Open the event, then Settings in the event menu.
  2. Under Visitor scan defaults, choose whether new visitors are enriched in full. When it is off, only a missing email is looked up.
  3. Choose whether the conversation starts capturing as soon as a visitor's page opens. Click Save defaults.
  4. Check the event details and business info.
  5. Under Standard questions, edit the list for this show. Add, reorder or remove questions, then click Save questions.

Good to know Changes to scan defaults apply to visitors added after you save.

Read the narration

Each event has its own settings. With the event selected, open Settings in the event menu.

Under visitor scan defaults, automatic enrichment looks up each new visitor's company and role in full. Turn it off if you only want a missing email looked up.

Auto start is for the conversation. Turn it on and the conversation starts capturing as soon as a new visitor's page opens. Click Save defaults.

The event details and business info are here too. Fix anything from setup that needs changing for this show.

Now the standard questions for this event. Reps see them in order during a conversation. Type over a question to change it.

Click Add question to add one just for this show, and type it in.

Use the arrows to reorder, or the bin to remove one. Click Save questions when you're done.

The event is ready. Your team can start adding visitors.
