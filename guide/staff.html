Booth staff guide

This guide takes a booth rep from first sign in to the first scan, and through the follow up afterwards. It takes about 10 minutes. Your admin sets up the event first. If you cannot see your event, ask them. If something goes wrong at the booth, the last section shows how to fix it.

Get ready

1

Connect Gmail or Outlook

Connect the email you work in, Gmail or Outlook. Follow up emails then go out from your own mailbox, the visitor sees your name, and every email lands in your Sent folder. If you do not connect, the email goes out from our no reply address and you are only copied in CC. Your calendar is also checked for clashes when you book a meeting.

  1. Open Integrations in the left sidebar.
  2. Pick Google for Gmail and Calendar, or Microsoft for Outlook and its calendar. You can connect one at a time.
  3. Click Connect, then Continue. Sign in with your work account and approve the access.

Good to know You only do this once. If you disconnect later, emails go back to the no reply address, with you in CC, and calendar checks stop until you connect again.

2

Complete your profile and signature

Your role, photo and phone numbers sit under your name in every follow up email.

  1. Open Team, find your name and click Edit.
  2. Add your role and your LinkedIn link.
  3. Upload a photo. A square or portrait headshot works best, up to 1 MB.
  4. Add your office phone, extension and mobile number.
  5. Click Save changes.

Good to know Your signature always uses your latest details.

Read the narration

Add your role and your LinkedIn link. Both go into your email signature.

Upload a photo. A square or portrait headshot works best, up to one megabyte.

Add your office phone, your extension and your mobile number. Visitors see them under your name in every follow-up.

Click Save changes. Your signature updates everywhere you send from, and it always uses your latest details.

At the booth

3

Scan your first badge

This is the one step that starts everything else.

  1. Open the event and go to Registrations. Click Add visitor.
  2. On your phone, tap Scan a badge or business card and hold it steady inside the frame.
  3. You can also upload a photo of the badge, or type the details. Name and company are required.
  4. Turn on Enrich new visitors automatically if you want the full profile pulled in. When it is off, only a missing email is looked up.
  5. Click Add visitor. The visitor's page opens straight away.

Good to know Add an email or phone number if you have one. It saves a lookup.

Read the narration

Here's how to add your first visitor at the show. Open the event, and go to Registrations.

The quickest way is to scan. On your phone, tap Scan a badge or business card, and hold it steady inside the frame.

4

Read the visitor page

The moment you scan a badge, this page opens. It is built to help you start the conversation warm, before you say a word.

  1. Check the header. It shows name, company, title, country and the ICP badge.
  2. Check their email with the visitor, so your follow up reaches them.
  3. Read the CRM row under the header. We check your CRM with that email and show your current relationship with the visitor in one row: the last touch, deal status, product focus, pain point, deal owner, and what is pending for the visitor and for your team.
  4. Open their LinkedIn profile and send a connection request while they are still at the booth. Copy LinkedIn message gives you a note to send.
  5. Read the conversation brief. These are your talking points, and the last one is a question to open with.
  6. Check the company summary. The questions to ask list shows the standard questions for this event, in order, whenever you need them.
  7. The conversation is analysed as it happens, and it starts on its own when you scan the badge. You see it in the live transcript on the left, with the questions asked, challenges, decisions and commitments. If the event is set not to start by default, press Start here.
  8. Watch the conversation score and the intent score build as the conversation goes on. When intent reaches a certain level, we nudge you to ask for a meeting. Book meeting sets up the calendar invite.
  9. When the conversation is over, rate the lead Hot, Warm or Cold, based on how it felt to you. You can change it at any time.
  10. Click Stop and create summary. You move to the Summary page automatically.

Good to know The next two steps cover the summary, the email and scheduling.

After the conversation

5

Approve the summary and get the email draft

Everything the app writes is yours to check before it reaches the visitor.

  1. After Stop and create summary, the Summary page opens on its own.
  2. Read the summary. It has highlights, decisions, open questions, next steps and action items. Edit anything that is wrong.
  3. Click Approve summary. The visitor is now ready for a follow up email.
  4. Open the Email tab and click Generate email.
  5. Read the subject and message, edit anything you like, and click Save changes.

Good to know The visitor never sees the summary. It is your record of the conversation.

6

Send the email or schedule it

Send it while the conversation is fresh, or choose the moment it lands.

  1. To send now, open the Email tab, read the draft and click Send email. You are copied on it.
  2. To schedule, open Communications from the event menu.
  3. Tick the visitors marked Ready and click Schedule.
  4. Choose a schedule and a send time, then click Confirm and Schedule.
  5. Open the Scheduled emails tab to see what is queued. To change your mind, tick the visitor and click Cancel scheduled.

Good to know A visitor is Ready when they have an email address and an approved summary.

Fix problems and edit details

One video for the problems you may meet at a show, and for fixing a visitor's details. It is split into chapters, and each chapter is under a minute. Each chapter starts with what you may see, and then the steps to fix it. We keep adding chapters to this video.

The most common problems

1

The microphone is blocked

The app needs your permission to use the microphone before it can record.

  1. Open the site settings for the app in your browser, and set Microphone to Allow.
  2. Open the privacy settings on your device, and make sure your browser is allowed to use the microphone.
  3. Come back to the visitor and tap Start recording.

Good to know On a phone, the browser has its own switch in the device privacy settings. Check both places.

2

Part of the recording did not upload

Sometimes the app tells you that part of your recording could not be uploaded.

  1. Contact support.
  2. Tell them which visitor the recording was for, and roughly when the conversation happened.

Good to know Do this soon after the conversation, while you remember the details.

3

The login code does not arrive

The app shows no error when a code is late. You just wait, so here is what to do.

  1. Look in your spam or junk folder.
  2. Check the email address is the one on your account.
  3. Wait a minute, then ask for a new code.
  4. Still nothing? Contact us, and we will help you in.

Good to know Ask for the new code once, then wait. Several requests in a row can make it slower.

Recording

4

No microphone found

The app did not find a microphone, or another app is using it.

  1. Plug in or switch on a microphone, and check the right one is chosen in your device sound settings.
  2. Close any other app that may be using the microphone, such as a call or a voice recorder.
  3. Tap Start recording again.

Good to know A call app running in the background is the usual cause.

5

The recording has not started

Nothing is captured until the timer runs, and there is no summary without a conversation.

  1. Look for the timer on the visitor page.
  2. If it is not running, tap Start recording.
  3. Talk as you normally would. Tap Stop and create summary when you are done.

Good to know If your event is set not to start recording by default, you always start it yourself.

6

No internet connection

The app needs an internet connection to record.

  1. Check your connection, or join a different network.
  2. Tap Start recording again.

Good to know Before the show, check the connection at your booth and keep a phone hotspot ready.

Fix a visitor's details

7

A badge was scanned with no details

You may decide later that you do want the full profile.

  1. Open the visitor.
  2. Tap Enrich visitor.
  3. Wait while the app fills in the profile. The page updates on its own.

Good to know It works in the middle of a conversation, right after it, and after a batch scan.

8

Wrong LinkedIn profile

The brief and the follow up are only as good as the profile behind them.

  1. Tap Search, next to LinkedIn. It opens a web search for the visitor's name and company.
  2. Open the right profile and copy its link.
  3. Tap the pencil next to LinkedIn, paste the link and tap Save.
  4. Tap Enrich visitor to fetch the details again.

Good to know Check the company on the profile before you copy it.

9

Wrong email address

A wrong address means the follow up never reaches the visitor.

  1. Check the right address with the visitor.
  2. Tap the pencil next to the email, type the correct address and tap Save.
  3. Tap Enrich visitor. The app checks your CRM again with the new address.

Good to know Do this while the visitor is still in front of you.

10

Change anything else

Sometimes the detail you want to fix is not the email or the LinkedIn link.

  1. Tap Edit on the visitor page.
  2. Change the contact details, the rating, the representative or the custom fields.
  3. Tap Save. If the person or the company changed, tap Enrich visitor again.

Good to know Edit opens the whole profile of the visitor, so you can fix everything in one go.

Email

11

There is no email draft yet

The email is written from your conversation, so it needs a conversation first.

  1. Record the conversation, or write the summary yourself.
  2. Read the summary and approve it.
  3. Open the Email tab and tap Generate email.

Good to know The draft appears only after a conversation has happened and its summary is approved.

12

The email did not send

It is usually a missing address, or a mailbox that needs connecting again.

  1. Check the To line. If the visitor has no email address, add it.
  2. If it still does not send, open Integrations and reconnect Gmail or Outlook.
  3. Come back and tap Send email again.

Good to know Gmail or Outlook sign in can expire. Reconnecting takes a minute.
